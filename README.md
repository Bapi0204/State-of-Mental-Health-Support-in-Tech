## Overall Learning 

This project examines mental health awareness, employee perceptions, and organizational support within the technology sector. Using survey data from employees across multiple countries, it analyzes workplace culture, mental health benefits, and comfort in seeking help. The study finds improved awareness but ongoing challenges such as stigma and unequal access to support. It highlights the need for proactive, inclusive, and culturally sensitive mental health policies in organizations.
